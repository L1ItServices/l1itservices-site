HELP & FEEDBACK

A direct line
to app support.

Have a question, found a bug, or want to share feedback about an L1 It Services app? Email support@l1itservices.ca.

Help us understand the issue

Include the following details if available:

  • The app name and version.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • What you were trying to do, what happened, and any steps that reproduce the problem.
  • A screenshot, with personal or sensitive information removed.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Our apps are in development. Release information for The Alchemist and Cornhole Score Keeper (working title) will appear on our website when available.
